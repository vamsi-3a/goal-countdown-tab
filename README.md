# Goal Countdown Tab

A Manifest V3 new-tab extension for Chrome and Brave. Open a new tab to see up to **three live goal countdowns** (days, hours, minutes, seconds) and a **favicon grid of favorite sites**. Goals and links are edited on the page and stored only on this device.

## Why this exists

The point is not another to-do list. It is **urgency you cannot ignore**.

We open the browser constantly. Most new tabs go to a blank page or a feed, and the deadline for a personal goal—finish a project, sit an exam, ship something that matters—stays out of sight until it is too late. This extension puts the clock on that page instead. Every new tab shows time still running: days, hours, minutes, seconds. Seeing it often is the reminder to stay on track.

Three goals is a limit on purpose. If everything is urgent, nothing is. Keep the few dates that actually matter in front of you, and treat the rest of the tab as a launch pad for the sites you already use.

## Load unpacked (Chrome or Brave)

1. Open `chrome://extensions` or `brave://extensions`.
2. Turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder (the one that contains `manifest.json`).
4. Open a new tab. You should see three goal cards and the links section.

### Brave new tab page

Brave sometimes keeps its own dashboard. If the extension UI does not appear:

1. Open `brave://settings/newTab`.
2. Turn off Brave’s New Tab Page (or choose the installed extension as the new tab page).
3. Open a new tab again.

## Use

- Click a card to set a **title** and **target date/time**. Timers tick every second while the tab is open.
- After the target time, the card shows **Goal reached**.
- **Clear goal** empties that slot (maximum three goals).
- **Add site** stores a name and URL (maximum 24). Click **Edit** on a tile to change or remove it. Click the tile itself to open the site.
